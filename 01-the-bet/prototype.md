# The Prototype Bet
## What I Built
<!-- One sentence: what does this prototype demonstrate? -->
A resolution-loop chat prototype: the assistant answers a banking request, classifies the outcome live (resolved / scoped out / deflected), and asks the customer to confirm — turning each dialogue into a labeled signal that improves resolution over time.

Automator — it resolves the customer's request end-to-end rather than assisting a human agent.
## The Bet in One Sentence
<!-- What you're building, for whom, why now -->
For BOG retail banking customers, an assistant whose moat is an outcome-labeled resolution loop on our own flows — not raw Georgian language — because that's the one advantage a foundation model can't ship natively.
## Kill Criteria
<!-- When would you stop? What evidence would kill this bet? -->
Kill it if the labeled loop doesn't move a real metric — e.g. after a few weeks, resolution rate (FSR) is flat versus a non-looped baseline, or the outcome labels don't hold up beyond the sessions they were tuned on. Also kill it if a base-model upgrade closes the quality gap without the loop — meaning the wedge was language, not the loop.
